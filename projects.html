Wamaina Gas Management System

A web system to replace the shop's paper records. Stock is tracked by size and brand, so the boss can check the day's sales from his phone, wherever he is.

  • Refill sales that record brand swaps both ways, so the shop knows who owes which cylinder
  • Tracks customer credit, prepaid orders and cylinders lent before an empty comes back
  • Daily cash reconciliation with Cash, M-Pesa and Till kept separate
  • Role-based access for the admin, store staff and delivery rider
