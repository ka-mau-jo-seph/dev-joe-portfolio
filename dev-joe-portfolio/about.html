I learn by building things people actually use.

An IT graduate turning a small business's daily problems into working software.

Since graduating in 2024, I've worked at Wamaina Gas Suppliers, an authorized gas dealer in Kwambira, Limuru. That job turned into my training ground as a developer.

Every day I see how a small business runs: cylinders counted by hand, customers on credit, brands swapped at the counter, expenses taken out of the day's cash. I first built the shop a website, and now I'm writing a system that handles all of that in one place.

It has taught me things a tutorial can't. I design database tables around real edge cases and talk to the people who'll use the software. I'm ready to bring that to a development team.
